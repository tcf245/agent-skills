# Resume Style Guide

Use this reference for Eric's resume work. Treat current document content and newer explicit decisions as authoritative when they conflict with this snapshot.

## Narrative Ladder

Keep each layer at its own level:

1. **Header**: identity and contact information.
2. **Summary**: professional positioning and repeatable capabilities.
3. **Experience**: concrete proof through ownership, scope, systems, and outcomes.
4. **Projects**: recent hands-on evidence that adds something the employment history does not.

Do not jump from a broad positioning sentence into a list of unrelated project metrics. Put TPS, DAU, success rates, and lead-time improvements beside the work that produced them.

## Bullet Style

Default pattern:

```text
Capability Label: Action + ownership or scope + result.
```

Prefer:

```text
Feed Optimization: Re-architected the content feed, reducing response time by 90% and driving a 20% increase in DAU.
```

Avoid:

```text
Worked on feed improvements and significantly improved performance.
```

Apply these rules:

- Start with a strong, accurate verb.
- Name the actual system, workflow, or business process.
- Connect architecture to why it mattered: scale, reliability, speed, extensibility, cost, revenue, adoption, or team throughput.
- Use one primary idea per bullet.
- Prefer concrete before/after values over adjectives.
- Define denominators and success-rate meanings.
- Avoid `responsible for`, generic collaboration claims, and internal acronyms without context.
- Keep labels short and parallel. Use engineering language for engineering achievements.

## Senior-Engineer Signals

Prioritize evidence of:

- turning ambiguous requirements into a validated production system;
- owning production reliability or investigation workflows;
- architecture and scale decisions;
- technical leadership and team multiplier effects;
- cross-functional product, engineering, operations, and data loops;
- measurable improvement to users, business outcomes, or engineering throughput.

Do not mistake technology lists for seniority. Technologies support the story; judgment and impact establish the level.

## Consistency Checklist

- Use `Experience`, not `Experiences`.
- Use `Present`, not `now`, in date ranges.
- Keep work date spacing consistent, for example `Mar 2018 – Aug 2022`.
- Use location form `City, Country (Hybrid)` or `City, Country (On-site)`.
- Use international phone formatting and remove domestic trunk prefixes after the country code.
- Normalize names such as `Java EE`, `Spring Boot`, `RocketMQ`, `ZooKeeper`, and `Distributed Systems`.
- Keep role descriptions in past tense for completed roles and current tense only for ongoing work.
- Use HTTPS link targets when available.
- Check hidden template characters and empty paragraphs in the exported PDF.
